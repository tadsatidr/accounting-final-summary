What this guide stays inside

The summaries follow the four lecture decks. Textbook wording is used only to make a rule the slides already teach easier to apply (for example, how a debit balance in the allowance changes the adjusting entry). Topics the slides do not teach are not turned into extra chapters. In particular:

  • Chapter 7 names natural resources on the chapter map and names fixed asset turnover in the learning outcomes. The slides do not teach depletion, and they do not compute fixed asset turnover. The formula sheet gives the standard ratio in one line and says so.
  • Bond accounting stops where the slides stop: issue price, effective-interest amortization, and retirement at maturity. There is no lesson on early extinguishment or on issuing bonds between interest dates.
  • LIFO is taught, and the slides also state that IFRS does not allow LIFO. Both points are kept.
  • The allowance chapter requires an expected-loss (allowance) model. A direct write-off of bad debts as they occur is marked as not acceptable.
